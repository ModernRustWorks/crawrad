The same, over standalone properties — this is the one tests reach.

Separated because [`MakepadCtl`] cannot be instantiated outside of a document, and what
matters to measure is the READING: that the text tracks the property, and that a value
the canvas cannot render stays blank instead of leaking its internal
representation onto the designer screen.
