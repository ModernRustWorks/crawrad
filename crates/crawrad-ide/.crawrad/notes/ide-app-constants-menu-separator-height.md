Height of the separator line between two menu blocks.

The height of the RULE between two blocks — the `CRAWRAD_MENU_SEPARADOR` of
the theme.

A separator that measured a whole row would leave a dead strip at the foot
of the popup, because the popup is sized to fit exactly.
