The panel that the argument names, or the refusal phrase.

Returns the phrase along with it, and not an `Option`, for the same reason as
[`page_control`]: on the other side of the protocol there is no window,
and whoever wrote `inspetor` instead of `inspector` needs to hear that instead
of watching the IDE do nothing.
