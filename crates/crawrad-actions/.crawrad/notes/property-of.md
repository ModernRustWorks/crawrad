Validates and returns the node and property key from action arguments.

The `node` and `key` an Inspector action names, CHECKED — or the sentence
that says what was wrong.

Both arguments are required and the refusal is STATED: opening "the editor
of nothing" is the blank window nobody knows where it came from. The node
must EXIST — a stale id from a closed document would write into a void —
and the key must be in its class's CATALOGUE, here and not in the shell,
because it applies equally to a click and to the protocol (ADR 0005).
